About me

Curious, hands-on, and learning fast.

“The best way to learn to build is to build, then ask why it works.”

I'm Judith, a new builder who uses AI as a pair programmer to go from a plain-language idea to a finished product.

I focus on understanding every step: why a layout behaves the way it does, how data moves through an app, and how to ask the right question when something breaks.

I care about clean design, clear writing, and software that feels considered rather than assembled.
